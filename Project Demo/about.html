About Pothchola

Your simple, safe and smart way to explore Bangladesh.

What is Pothchola?

Pothchola is a travel web app that helps you discover and book curated trips across Bangladesh—like Cox’s Bazar, Sylhet, Bandarban, Rangamati, and Tanguar Haor. We keep things clear and transparent so you can focus on the journey.

How our packages are designed

  • Trip length: Designed for 3 calendar days with 2 overnight stays.
  • Package tiers: Basic, Standard, Premium (amenities and availability vary).
  • Pricing breakdown: Transport (≈ 700–2000 Tk) + Accommodation (Single base ≈ 2500–6000 Tk; Double ×1.5; Family ×2.25) + Full board (≈ 3000–9000 Tk).
  • Room types: Single (1 person), Double (2 persons), Family (3–4 persons max).

(These ranges reflect typical inclusions; actual totals are shown live on each package.)

Services we provide

  • End-to-end booking: transport, accommodation, and full board.
  • Curated stays and activities matched to each destination.
  • Real-time package availability and cost calculation.
  • Local guidance and tips for a stress-free trip.

Safety & Security

  • Verified service providers and partner accommodations.
  • Clear pricing with no hidden fees.
  • Secure data handling for your account details.
  • Instant booking confirmation saved in your profile.
